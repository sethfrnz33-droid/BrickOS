# Brick Tuner Suite DSi v32

- Adds an RPM-triggered Kevin Jonas alert mode.
- When live RPM exceeds 5000, the app flashes a full-screen Kevin Jonas image on both screens.
- Flash frequency ramps from 1 Hz at 5000 RPM to 10 Hz at 6000 RPM, then clamps.
- Plays the bundled `nierniernier.pcm8` clip on each flash onset while the alert is active.
- Assets live on the SD card under `/BrickECU/kevinjonas.rgb565` and `/BrickECU/nierniernier.pcm8`.

DSi-only change. 3DS files remain untouched.
