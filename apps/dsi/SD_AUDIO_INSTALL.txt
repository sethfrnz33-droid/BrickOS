AUDIO INSTALLATION ON REAL DSi SD CARD

Copy all files from this project's DSi/SD_Files/BrickECU/sfx/
to your physical DSi SD card: BrickECU/sfx/

Expected 14 files: 00.pcm8, 01.pcm8, ... 13.pcm8.
The .nds ROM does not contain these audio files.
Restart the DSi app after copying so the cached audio readiness check runs again.

Press A on a sound in SOUNDBOARD to trigger playback and see its diagnostic.
